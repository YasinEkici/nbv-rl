## Reproduction

Use `uv sync --extra demo` and the commands in [DEMO_GUIDE](../../docs/DEMO_GUIDE.md). Palette generation additionally uses `uv sync --extra demo --group palette`; rendering and viewing do not require colorspacious. `scripts/report_figures.py` and `scripts/report_slides.py` generate new v2 exports and refuse to overwrite existing versioned files. Reproduce each exporter in its own clean checkout of the render_commit recorded in its sidecar, before the generated v2 files were committed. Delivered versioned files are never overwritten.
